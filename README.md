# Privacy Policy – Habit Rewards

Effective Date: February 2026

Habit Rewards ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

- Name
- Email address
- UPI ID (for reward payments)
- Task activity data
- Referral activity data
- Device information (basic usage analytics)

We do NOT collect:
- Bank account numbers
- ATM or debit card details
- Credit card information
- OTPs or passwords

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Track task completion and rewards
- Process redemption requests
- Verify payouts
- Improve app performance
- Prevent fraud and misuse

Your UPI ID is used ONLY for sending approved redemption payments by the admin.

## 3. Rewards & Payments

Users earn coins by completing daily tasks or advertiser campaigns.

Coins may be redeemed for cash via UPI. All payouts are manually reviewed and approved by the admin.

Habit Rewards does not charge users any fee for participation.

If anyone contacts you asking for money related to redemption, please ignore and report it.

## 4. Advertiser Campaigns

The app may include third-party game or bonus campaigns provided by advertisers.

Users are not required to make any payment to participate.

We are not responsible for third-party apps downloaded through campaigns.

## 5. Data Protection

We use secure cloud services to store user data. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Habit Rewards is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected within the app.
